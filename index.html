About WADDLE

A student-developed e-learning platform of Pateros Technological College.

How WADDLE Started

WADDLE, which stands for Workplace Administration & Digital Development Learning Environment, was developed by five fourth-year Bachelor of Science in Office Administration students of Pateros Technological College as part of their COMP 4 project.

The group created WADDLE to provide Office Administration students with an accessible learning platform where they can develop essential digital skills, gain practical knowledge, and prepare for real workplace responsibilities.

WADDLE helps learners build practical digital skills and prepare for future administrative roles.

Purpose

To help Office Administration students develop essential digital skills and practical knowledge for common administrative tasks.

Mission

To provide accessible, learner-centered, and practical digital learning experiences that strengthen students' office administration skills and workplace readiness.

Vision

To become a helpful e-learning platform that empowers future Office Administration professionals to become competent, confident, and digitally skilled individuals.

Goals

  • Develop digital and administrative skills.
  • Provide organized and self-paced modules.
  • Strengthen practical knowledge through activities and assessments.
  • Build confidence in using digital tools.
  • Prepare learners for administrative careers.

Value Proposition

WADDLE offers organized e-learning modules, practical activities, and assessments that help learners apply digital tools effectively and prepare for future administrative roles.

Learning Philosophy

WADDLE promotes learner-centered, competency-based, and self-paced learning through activities, tests, and work-play scenarios.
